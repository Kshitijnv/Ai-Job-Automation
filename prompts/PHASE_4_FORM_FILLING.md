# Phase 4 — Smart Form Filling

Implement only this phase.

---

## Goal

Automatically fill known application fields.

---

## Inputs

* profile.json
* generated resume
* portal mapping

---

## Auto-fill

* Name
* Email
* Phone
* Experience
* Notice Period
* Location
* Resume Upload

---

## Sensitive Fields

Do not auto-submit:

* Salary
* Visa
* Disability
* Veteran
* Diversity

Collect them for later review.

---

## Success Criteria

Known fields fill automatically without user interaction.
